# Procurement Policy: Delivery Lead Times and Stock Criticality

## Policy ID: POL-OPS-003
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Scope:** Factory Floor Operational Continuity

### 1. Delivery Tolerance
- For factory assembly lines, the maximum acceptable delivery lead time for shortage mitigation is **7 business days**.
- Suppliers quoting delivery lead times > 7 business days are considered unviable for acute shortages unless expedited courier freight is negotiated.

### 2. Minimum Order Safety Quantity
- The agent must order quantity equal to the deficit: `Target Safety Stock - Current Stock`.
- Orders must not exceed 2x the standard replenishment lot size without supervisory authorization.

### 3. Stock Out Handling
- When stock reaches 0 units and zero alternative approved suppliers have available inventory, the agent must immediately transition to `BLOCKED` state and notify the human factory plant manager with an urgent alert.
