# Procurement Policy: Spending Limits and Approval Thresholds

## Policy ID: POL-FIN-001
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Scope:** Autonomous Factory Procurement and Restocking Orders

### 1. Automatic Ordering Threshold
- **Maximum Autonomous Limit:** Purchases with a total estimated cost (including item unit cost, shipping, and applicable tariffs) of **less than or equal to $10,000.00 USD** may proceed without manual executive sign-off, provided all supplier qualification and inventory urgency criteria are satisfied.
- **Strict Hard Ceiling:** No agent, workflow, or override instruction may execute an automated order exceeding $10,000.00 USD.

### 2. High-Value Escalation ($10,000.00+ USD)
- Any proposed order where total calculated cost strictly exceeds **$10,000.00 USD** must be placed in `APPROVAL_REQUIRED` status.
- A human Procurement Officer or Plant Operations Director must review the quote, delivery terms, and justification before simulated or live purchase order generation.
- The agent must generate a full impact analysis and cost breakdown for the human reviewer.
